Do MyWhoosh rides count toward Garmin Training Load?

Sometimes the ride shows up in Garmin Connect but Training Load, Training Status or Recovery do not move. This page explains why that happens and what you can do about it, honestly.

The short answer

Garmin calculates its training metrics from activities it trusts: rides recorded on a Garmin device, and rides from a small set of partner platforms that feed Garmin's Unified Training Status (Garmin's support site names this feature and its supported third-party platforms). MyWhoosh is not one of those partners, and a plain FIT file import is not the same thing. So a MyWhoosh ride that reaches Garmin Connect as a file import often appears in your activity list without moving Training Load, Training Status or Recovery.

Source: Garmin Support, "When Will My 3rd Party Activities Show Garmin Training Metrics?"

Why riders get confused

  • It used to work for some people. Riders report that older imports once counted and then stopped, after Garmin changed how it treats uploaded files. Community threads in r/mywhoosh and on the Garmin forums describe exactly this.
  • The ride looks complete. Distance, power and heart rate are all there, so it feels like the metrics should follow. They are separate decisions Garmin makes about the file's source, not about its contents.
  • Friends on Zwift see their metrics move. Zwift is a supported Garmin partner platform; MyWhoosh is not. Same workout, different treatment.

What you can do

  • Record on a Garmin device while you ride. This is the dependable route to the metrics your device and sensors normally provide. It means starting and stopping two recordings per ride.
  • Import the FIT file by hand if you only need the ride in your history. Free, works for occasional rides, and may leave the training metrics untouched.
  • Use a sync app like ours. MyWhoosh 2 Garmin labels the uploaded ride with the Garmin device model you choose. Garmin decides which training metrics it calculates from any uploaded ride, and a successful upload does not guarantee a particular metric. We are validating exactly which metrics synced rides feed and will update this page when we know.

Common questions

What about the fitfiletools device changer?

It rewrites a FIT file so it looks recorded by a Garmin device, one file at a time, from a computer. It is a legitimate manual workaround some riders use. Our app applies the same idea automatically on your phone; the same caveat applies to both: Garmin decides what it calculates, and nothing is guaranteed.

Will MyWhoosh become an official partner?

That is MyWhoosh's and Garmin's decision, not something a third-party app can influence. If an official integration ships, use it.

Does this affect Strava?

No. Strava calculates its own numbers from whatever it receives. This page is only about Garmin Connect's training metrics.
