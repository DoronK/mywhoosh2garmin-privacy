MyWhoosh 2 Garmin · Android

Your MyWhoosh rides,
in Garmin Connect.

A free, unofficial Android utility that copies your MyWhoosh rides to Garmin Connect, without exporting and importing every FIT file by hand.

Get it on Google Play

Android only. You need MyWhoosh and Garmin Connect accounts.

Know what you are connecting. This app needs sign-in credentials for both services. It is not an official integration and is not affiliated with or endorsed by MyWhoosh or Garmin. Read the privacy policy before deciding whether to use it.

What it does, and what it doesn't

The app downloads a ride's FIT file from MyWhoosh, labels it with the Garmin device model you select, and uploads it to Garmin Connect. You can sync a ride manually or enable background checks for new rides.

Uploading a ride is not the same as updating every Garmin metric. Do not rely on imported rides to update Training Status, Acute Load, VO2 max or Recovery Time. Those outcomes have not been verified for this app. Record with your Garmin device if you need the metrics that device normally provides.

Sync uses unofficial service connections, so changes at MyWhoosh or Garmin can interrupt it. Background timing is controlled by Android and can be delayed by battery settings. This is a best-effort utility, not an instant or guaranteed sync service.

Set up your first sync

  1. Install the Android app. Use the Google Play listing. You need a MyWhoosh account with a recorded ride and a Garmin Connect account.
  2. Connect MyWhoosh in Settings. Sign in with the account that holds your rides. If MyWhoosh says you are already logged in on another device, see the help below.
  3. Connect Garmin in Settings. If Garmin asks for a verification code, enter it in the app. Never share the code with the developer or post it online.
  4. Choose the ride device. Select the Garmin device model the uploaded ride should be labelled with. This changes the file's device label. It does not mean the ride was recorded on that physical device or guarantee extra Garmin metrics.
  5. Sync one ride and check Garmin Connect. Confirm its date, duration and available ride data before syncing more. If you already record the same ride on your watch or bike computer, check for duplicates in Garmin and any connected services.
  6. Turn on background sync only if you want it. Android decides when background checks run. Open the app and use manual sync when you need to check a ride now.

Your sign-in details

The app stores credentials and session tokens on your phone and uses them to sign in to MyWhoosh and Garmin. No server run by the developer receives your credentials or rides. A small developer-hosted version check also runs at launch; the privacy policy explains that request and the storage details.

This is a description of how the app works, not an independent security audit. If you are not comfortable giving an unofficial app these credentials, do not connect your accounts. You can disconnect an account in Settings to remove its saved credentials and session from the app.

When a sync doesn't work

MyWhoosh says I am already logged in on another device

Sign out of MyWhoosh on any device where the game is still signed in. Wait 15-30 minutes, then try connecting this app before opening the game again. Avoid repeated sign-in attempts during that wait. The error comes from MyWhoosh's session handling, not a missing ride.

The ride has no downloadable FIT file

This app needs the file supplied by MyWhoosh. Check whether that ride has a FIT download on the MyWhoosh website. If it does not, the app cannot create one. Do not keep retrying the same missing file.

Garmin asks for upload consent or pauses uploads

Sign in to the same Garmin Connect account and follow any consent request it shows. If Garmin is temporarily refusing uploads, wait rather than repeatedly pressing Sync. If you cannot find a consent request, ask Garmin support. The app cannot grant consent or remove an account restriction for you.

The ride appears twice in another connected service

The same ride may arrive through both MyWhoosh's connection and Garmin's connection. Review those links before syncing more. Keep the route you want, and check carefully before deleting any activity. Do not disconnect a service unless you understand which other activities use it.

I still need help
