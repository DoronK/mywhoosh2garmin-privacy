How to get MyWhoosh rides into Garmin Connect.

MyWhoosh does not send rides to Garmin Connect by itself. There are three ways to do it, and each one has honest trade-offs. This page compares them so you can pick the one that fits how you ride.

Option 1: export and import each ride by hand

Export the FIT file from MyWhoosh (or from Strava, if your MyWhoosh account is linked there), then import it in Garmin Connect on the web with Import Data.

Cost: free, no extra app. Limits: you repeat this for every ride, and the ride may appear in Garmin Connect without updating the training metrics you expect. Check the imported activity and your device rather than assuming every metric changed.

Option 2: MyWhoosh 2 Garmin (free Android app)

MyWhoosh 2 Garmin is a free, unofficial Android utility built for exactly this gap. It downloads your MyWhoosh rides and uploads them to Garmin Connect, labelled with the Garmin device model you choose (Edge, Fenix, Forerunner and more). Garmin decides which training metrics it calculates from an uploaded ride; a successful upload does not guarantee a particular metric.

Get it on Google Play

Android only. You need MyWhoosh and Garmin Connect accounts.

Honest limits. The app needs your MyWhoosh and Garmin sign-in; credentials stay encrypted on your phone and no developer server sees them. It is unofficial and not affiliated with or endorsed by MyWhoosh or Garmin. Background sync timing is controlled by Android. It does not promise Training Status, Acute Load, VO2 max or Recovery Time, and no particular training metric is guaranteed from an uploaded ride. The setup and sync help page covers the full steps and limits.

Option 3: record on your Garmin device at the same time

Record with a compatible Garmin device and sensors while you ride in MyWhoosh if you need the metrics that setup normally provides. Results depend on your device, sensors and Garmin's requirements.

Cost: nothing extra if you already own the device. Limits: you start and stop two recordings for every ride, and if you also sync or import the MyWhoosh version you can end up with duplicates to clean up.

Which should you pick?

  • A ride or two, once in a while: manual export is fine.
  • Regular MyWhoosh riding with an Android phone: the app removes the weekly export chore.
  • You need the metrics your Garmin setup normally provides: dual-record with a compatible device and sensors.
