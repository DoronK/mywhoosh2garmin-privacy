Privacy Policy — MyWhoosh 2 Garmin

Effective date: November 4, 2026

MyWhoosh 2 Garmin ("the app") is an independent, unofficial utility that copies your cycling activities from MyWhoosh into Garmin Connect. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garmin Ltd. or MyWhoosh. Using it is subject to Garmin's and MyWhoosh's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policies for how each of them handles your data once it reaches their servers.

In short: no server run by the developer ever receives your credentials, rides or any account data. The app runs on your device and talks directly to MyWhoosh's and Garmin's own official servers — the same ones their own apps use. The only developer-hosted request is a small version-config check at launch (see "Where it's sent").

What the app accesses

  • Your MyWhoosh email/username and password — used only to sign in to MyWhoosh's own API on your behalf, so the app can fetch your ride list.
  • Your Garmin Connect email and password — used only to sign in to Garmin's own official mobile login flow (the same OAuth1/OAuth2 process the real Garmin Connect app uses), so the app can upload activities on your behalf.
  • Your ride data from MyWhoosh — title, date, duration, distance, power, heart rate, cadence, and calories, for whichever activities you choose to sync. This is read from MyWhoosh and written to Garmin Connect; the app doesn't collect or retain it anywhere beyond your device.
  • App settings — which Garmin device model to present activities as (Garmin Connect requires activities to claim a real Garmin device before it will calculate Training Load and Training Effect for them) and your auto-sync preferences.

Where it's stored

Everything above stays on your device only:

  • Passwords and session tokens are written exclusively to your platform's secure, encrypted storage — Keychain on iOS/macOS, encrypted EncryptedSharedPreferences (AndroidX Security/Jetpack) on Android. If that secure storage is ever unavailable, the app does not fall back to storing passwords in plain text — it drops them and asks you to re-enter them instead.
  • Nothing is backed up off-device: Android backups are disabled for this app, and iOS Keychain items are not synced via iCloud Keychain.
  • Sync logs shown in the app (connection results, errors) never include your password.

Where it's sent

The app sends network requests only to:

  • MyWhoosh's servers (mywhoosh.com and its API subdomains) — to log in and fetch your ride list.
  • The developer's version-config file (hosted on Supabase) — fetched on every launch so the app can tell whether a required update exists. The request carries no account, credential or ride data, but like any web request it exposes your IP address and basic request metadata to that host's logs.
  • Garmin's servers (garmin.com and its sso./connectapi. subdomains) — to log in and upload activities, using Garmin's own public mobile-app OAuth credentials (not anything specific to you or this app).

All requests use HTTPS. The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any other third-party SDK, and it does not send your credentials or ride data to any server the developer operates or controls.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account at any time from Settings — this clears that account's stored password and cached session from your device immediately.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it has stored — since nothing is held on a server, uninstalling is equivalent to a full data deletion. There's no separate "delete my account" request to make, because there's no account on our end to delete.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect information from anyone under 13.
